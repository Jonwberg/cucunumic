## 1 · Thesis

A 30-key wellness boutique on **11 hectares** of Pacific-coast Baja California Sur, opened complete with the full integrated wellness program. Brand-led narrative — *Wellness Destination* — grounded in the economic case for premium ADR, rich attach revenue, and disciplined demand-pulled expansion.

> **Cucunumic is a Pacific-coast wellness destination, opened complete. A 30-key boutique with full integrated wellness from day one. Phase 2 (40–50 keys) unlocks when guest demand pulls us beyond 30.**

### 1.1 Why it makes economic sense to invest

| Driver | Number / mechanism | Why it earns the premium |
|---|---|---|
| **ADR positioning** | $510 base ADR target | Los Cabos market $473; scenic-route segment $802. Cucunumic sits between, defensibly. |
| **Wellness attach-rate** | $600/ORN at stabilization (~63% of room revenue) | Wellness packages multiply RevPAR. A 30-key with 60% package penetration earns like a 50-key conventional resort. |
| **Lower seasonality** | Health travel less peak-driven | Smooths Cabo's high/low swing; better trailing-12 stability for the lender. |
| **Repeat-rate compounding** | 35–50% wellness vs 15–20% standard luxury | CAC declines year-over-year; brand defensibility increases with cohort age. |
| **First-mover position** | No clinical-grade wellness in BCS Pacific corridor | Paradero proves the segment in Todos Santos at $700–800 ADR. Cucunumic owns the wellness-anchored slot. |
| **Capital efficiency** | Open complete, no relaunch cost | Staged openings burn 2× the marketing spend and fragment brand identity. One launch, one story. |
| **Land appreciation** | 11 escriturado hectares on Pacific coast BCS | 8–12% annual land appreciation per Los Cabos benchmarks; asset value compounds independent of operations. |
| **Earned expansion** | Phase 2 only when guests are pulling us forward | Investors are buying a 30-key business with a free option on a second tranche the *market* funds the case for. |

### 1.2 Anchor lines for slide copy

- *"A wellness destination, opened complete."*
- *"30 keys, $510 ADR, 60% wellness attach. The math works at 30."*
- *"Phase 2 happens when guests demand it — not when we decide to build it."*

### 1.3 Brand thesis is downstream of economics

Cucunumic is a wellness destination *because* that positioning earns the ADR, the attach-rate, the repeat rate, and the lower seasonality the model needs. Brand and economics are the same argument, told from two ends.

### 2.3 Gate — Phase 1 to Phase 2 unlock

All four conditions must be simultaneously true. The slowest-binding condition determines the date.

1. **Trailing-12 ADR ≥ $510**
2. **Occupancy ≥ 65%** for 4 consecutive quarters
3. **Documented turnaway demand ≥ 15% of inquiries** during 2 consecutive peak seasons (Nov–Apr) — *this is typically the binding constraint*
4. **Modeled DSCR ≥ 1.5×** on Phase 2 expansion debt at the trigger date

If the gate doesn't clear, the project holds at 30 keys. Phase 1 LPs keep their position; nothing forces an expansion the market hasn't earned.

### 2.4 Capital structure

- **Two separate raises** (Phase 1 raise now; Phase 2 raise after gate clears).
- Phase 1 LPs hold **non-dilutable Phase 1 equity** — their stake in the 30-key business is preserved.
- Phase 2 raise at **uplifted valuation** based on Phase 1 stabilized cash flow (target uplift 1.6–2.0× Phase 1 entry valuation if gate clears on plan).
- Phase 1 LPs receive **pro-rata participation rights** in Phase 2 (option, not obligation).
- New Phase 2 capital can come from new LPs, hospitality construction debt, or a mix.

### 2.5 Land contribution

Capitalized at **preferred / negotiated value** between sponsor and LPs (typical family-and-friends round structure). Avoids the cost and timing of an independent appraisal. Specific value to be set at raise structuring.

## 6 · Phase 2 Capital Raise — Time-to-Close

### 6.1 Gate clearance timing

| Criterion | Earliest possible clearance |
|---|---|
| Trailing-12 ADR ≥ $510 | Y3 Q3 (mid-2030) |
| Occupancy ≥ 65% × 4Q | Y3 Q4 (end 2030) |
| **Turnaway ≥ 15% × 2 peak seasons** | **Y4 Q2 (Apr 2031)** — binding |
| DSCR ≥ 1.5× modeled | Y3 Q4 — not binding in success scenarios |

**Earliest realistic gate clearance: Apr 2031** (Y4 Q2). In aggressive ramp scenarios, ~12 months earlier.

### 6.2 Raise process timeline

| Phase | Months from gate clearance |
|---|---|
| Pre-marketing during Y3 H2 | -6 to 0 |
| Formal launch (gate-clearance day) | 0 |
| Initial diligence | 0 to 2 |
| Term sheet | 2 to 3 |
| Documentation | 3 to 5 |
| First close | 5 to 6 |
| Final close | 6 to 9 |

**Standard total: 6–9 months from gate to capital deployable.**

### 6.3 Accelerators that compress the timeline to ~3 months

| Accelerator | Time saved | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 LPs sign pro-rata rights at Phase 1 closing | 1–2 months | Legal complexity in Phase 1 docs |
| Pre-engage debt provider during Y3 | 2–3 months | Some fees during diligence |
| Phase 2 footprint pre-permitted with Phase 1 | 4–6 months | $50–100K extra A&E + permit work in Y0 |
| Audited Y2 + Y3 financials by Big-4 / equivalent | 1–2 months | $40–80K annual audit cost |

With all accelerators: **first close within 60 days** of gate clearance.

### 6.4 Variance under different ramp speeds

| Ramp scenario | Gate clears | Phase 2 close | Phase 2 opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggressive (12mo ahead) | Apr 2030 | Aug 2030 | early 2032 |
| Base case | Apr 2031 | Jul 2031 | early 2033 |
| Conservative (12mo behind) | Apr 2032 | Jan 2033 | early 2035 |
