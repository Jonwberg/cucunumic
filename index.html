Act II — The Place

04 — The Architecture of Healing

A constellation of cells,
not a single building.

Cucunumic is conceived as a wellness center where each therapeutic modality inhabits its own monolithic structure. The buildings do not contain the program — they are the program. A guest crosses the desert garden and arrives at one cell at a time: hyperbaric, sauna, infrared, cryo, float, sound. Each is a separate room with its own door, its own sky, its own silence.

A formal portico at the threshold — the Reception — collects guests, briefs them, and dispatches them into the circuit. The architecture is rammed earth, lime stucco, deep eaves, raised floors. Vernacular in material; ceremonial in geometry.
